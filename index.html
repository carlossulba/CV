Hi, I'm Carlos

Robotics, Cognition, Intelligence student at TUM, and research assistant at Science of Intelligence.

I'm currently studying Robotics, Cognition, Intelligence at the Technical University of Munich, and working as a research assistant at Science of Intelligence, where I build Unity environments for experiments on observational learning in agents. I'm endlessly curious about how robots perceive the world around them, and how they can collaborate better — both with each other and with the humans they work alongside.

I try to bring that curiosity into everything I work on, and I'm always looking for the next thing to learn, build, or improve. It's a mindset that keeps me progressively pushing my career forward, one project at a time.

Outside of robotics, you'll usually find me at the archery range, or in the kitchen brewing kombucha and kefir.
